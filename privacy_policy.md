## 5\.2 Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) — Push Notifications
*ℹ️  Firebase FCM is used solely to deliver push notifications. Google receives your device token to route notifications to your device.*

ABM uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), a notification delivery service provided by Google LLC, to send you push notifications regarding:

- **New messages received via in-app chat;**
- **Auction activity (new bids, auction ending soon, auction results);**
- **Listing status updates (approved, rejected);**
- **Deal Via Admin status updates and Admin decisions;**
- **Account security and moderation notices.**

To deliver push notifications, Firebase FCM assigns your device a unique FCM registration token. ABM stores this token in association with your account. Google's FCM infrastructure processes the routing of notifications to your device.

Data shared with Firebase: FCM device token, notification payload (message content and routing metadata). Notification content is transmitted securely.

You may disable push notifications at any time through your device's notification settings or within the ABM app settings. Disabling notifications does not affect your account.

Firebase Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

# 6\. Chat Messages: Encryption & Admin Access
**⚠️  Your in-app chat messages are NOT end-to-end encrypted. Admins can read stored messages for moderation purposes.**

ABM's in-app chat system provides transport-layer encryption (TLS/SSL) for messages in transit between your device and ABM's servers. This means messages are protected from interception while being transmitted over the network.

However, ABM does NOT implement end-to-end encryption. This means:

- **Once messages are delivered and stored on ABM's backend database (Appwrite), they are stored in a readable format;**
- **Authorized ABM administrators have the technical capability and the explicit right to read stored chat messages;**
- **This access is exercised specifically for the purposes of: investigating scam reports; resolving disputes under Deal Via Admin; moderating violations of the Terms & Conditions; and complying with lawful orders from Pakistani authorities.**

This approach is intentional and mirrors the moderation model employed by major classifieds platforms (such as OLX Pakistan) to protect users from fraud and abuse. By using ABM's chat feature, you expressly consent to this level of access.

Do not send content via ABM chat that you wish to keep entirely private, including sensitive financial details or personal identification documents.
