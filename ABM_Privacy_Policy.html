1. Introduction

AB Marketplace (ABM) ("ABM", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of its users ("you" or "User"). This Privacy Policy describes in detail what personal information we collect, how it is used and shared, how it is protected, and your rights with respect to that information.

ABM is a hybrid marketplace platform combining classifieds (OLX-style peer-to-peer listings), multi-vendor product discovery (Daraz-style retail), and auction/bidding functionality (eBay-style), operating exclusively within the Islamic Republic of Pakistan. By creating an account or using the ABM App, you consent to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.

 

2. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for your personal information is ABM Operations, operating within Pakistan. For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at: abconputers@gmail.com

 

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you register and use ABM, you voluntarily provide us with:

• Account Information: Full name, username, phone number, and email address used during registration;

• Profile Information: Profile photograph, seller/buyer biography, and contact preferences;

• Listing Content (UGC): Item titles, descriptions, condition details, pricing, and photographs you upload when creating a listing;

• Communication Data: The content of messages, offers, and communications sent via ABM's in-app chat system;

• Dispute & Report Data: Information you submit when filing a scam report or dispute, including evidence (photographs, transaction proofs);

• Location Data: Your approximate or precise geographic location used for listing visibility, fraud detection, and province-locking anti-fraud measures. Location is collected via GPS (with your consent) or approximated via IP address.

 

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the App, certain information is collected automatically:

• Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (Android ID);

• Usage Data: Features accessed, pages viewed, time spent, actions taken within the App, auction bid history, and search queries;

• Log Data: IP address, app crash reports, error logs, and timestamp data collected for debugging and operational stability;

• Advertising Identifiers: Your Google Advertising ID (GAID) as collected by Google AdMob for the purpose of serving targeted advertisements (detailed in Section 5).

 

3.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

ABM does not collect:

• Credit card numbers, bank account details, or any payment credentials: (Premium billing is handled externally, off-app);

• Government-issued ID numbers: (e.g., CNIC) unless voluntarily submitted as part of a deal dispute investigation;

• Biometric data of any kind.

 

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for the following purposes:

• Account Management: Creating, verifying, and maintaining your user account;

• Listing & Marketplace Operations: Displaying your listings to other users, enabling search and discovery, and managing auction and barter features;

• Communication Facilitation: Enabling in-app messaging between buyers and sellers;

• Fraud Prevention & Safety: Detecting, investigating, and preventing scams, fake listings, shill bidding, and other prohibited activities. This includes using location data for province-locking anti-fraud checks;

• Content Moderation: Reviewing listings, chat messages, and user content to enforce our Terms & Conditions;

• Dispute Resolution: Reviewing submitted evidence to adjudicate Deal Via Admin disputes;

• Advertising: Displaying personalized and non-personalized ads via Google AdMob (see Section 5);

• Analytics & Product Improvement: Understanding how users interact with the App to improve features, performance, and user experience (see Section 6 on PostHog);

• Notifications: Sending push notifications related to your listings, bids, messages, and account activity (see Section 7 on Firebase);

• Legal Compliance: Complying with applicable Pakistani law, including the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA) 2016 and any lawful orders from Pakistani authorities.

5.2 Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) — Push Notifications

ℹ️ Firebase FCM is used solely to deliver push notifications. Google receives your device token to route notifications to your device.

ABM uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), a notification delivery service provided by Google LLC, to send you push notifications regarding:

• New messages received via in-app chat;

• Auction activity (new bids, auction ending soon, auction results);

• Listing status updates (approved, rejected);

• Deal Via Admin status updates and Admin decisions;

• Account security and moderation notices.

To deliver push notifications, Firebase FCM assigns your device a unique FCM registration token. ABM stores this token in association with your account. Google's FCM infrastructure processes the routing of notifications to your device.

Data shared with Firebase: FCM device token, notification payload (message content and routing metadata). Notification content is transmitted securely.

You may disable push notifications at any time through your device's notification settings or within the ABM app settings. Disabling notifications does not affect your account.

Firebase Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

 

5.3 PostHog (Product Analytics & Logging)

ABM uses PostHog, a product analytics platform, to collect anonymized usage data and application event logs. This helps us understand feature usage patterns, diagnose errors, and improve the App.

Data collected by PostHog includes:

ABM does not intentionally send your name, phone number, email address, or message content to PostHog. Event data is used exclusively for product analytics and service improvement.

PostHog Privacy Policy: https://posthog.com/privacy

6. Chat Messages: Encryption & Admin Access

⚠️ Your in-app chat messages are NOT end-to-end encrypted. Admins can read stored messages for moderation purposes.

ABM's in-app chat system provides transport-layer encryption (TLS/SSL) for messages in transit between your device and ABM's servers. This means messages are protected from interception while being transmitted over the network.

However, ABM does NOT implement end-to-end encryption. This means:

• Once messages are delivered and stored on ABM's backend database (Appwrite), they are stored in a readable format;

• Authorized ABM administrators have the technical capability and the explicit right to read stored chat messages;

• This access is exercised specifically for the purposes of: investigating scam reports; resolving disputes under Deal Via Admin; moderating violations of the Terms & Conditions; and complying with lawful orders from Pakistani authorities.

This approach is intentional and mirrors the moderation model employed by major classifieds platforms (such as OLX Pakistan) to protect users from fraud and abuse. By using ABM's chat feature, you expressly consent to this level of access.

Do not send content via ABM chat that you wish to keep entirely private, including sensitive financial details or personal identification documents.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active and as long as is necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Specifically:

9. Data Security

ABM implements commercially reasonable technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include:

• Transport Layer Security (TLS/SSL) encryption for all data transmitted between your device and our servers;

• Access controls restricting administrator access to personal data;

• Secure credential storage using hashing and salting for passwords;

No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach affecting your rights and freedoms, we will notify affected users as required by applicable law.
